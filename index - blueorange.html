Our Platform

What We're
Fighting For

01
Fair Teacher Pay

Our teachers deserve a living wage. Without competitive salaries, AAPS cannot retain and attract the talented educators our children need. A vote for Aaron is a vote for higher teacher salaries.

02
Disability Services

For too long this district has failed our most vulnerable community members. We must end the culture of disinvestment and build a district that truly serves every student — including those with disabilities.

03
World-Class Education

Every Ann Arbor child deserves the world-class education this district is capable of delivering. We will stop the exodus of families to other districts and reclaim our position as the best in Michigan.

About Aaron

A Pioneer,
For AAPS

My name is Aaron J. Puno and I'm a proud product of Ann Arbor Public Schools. I graduated from Pioneer High School in 2023 after thirteen happy years in this district — years that gave me everything I needed to mount this serious effort for public office.

Many of you may know me as editor-in-chief of the Pioneer Optimist, where I learned to listen, ask hard questions, and hold institutions accountable. Now I want to hold our school board to the same standard.

The world-class education I received here should be a fulfilled birthright of every child in this city — not a dream deferred. I know what's at stake because this district shaped me. I'm ready to fight for it.
